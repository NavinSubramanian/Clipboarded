Privacy Policy for ClipBoarded

ClipBoarded does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All information stored in the extension is saved locally on the user's device using Chrome's storage API. No data is sent to external servers, and no analytics or tracking tools are used.

The extension does not access browsing history, website content, or user activity beyond what is required for its functionality.
